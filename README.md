# Ease

**Prepare calmly. Perform confidently.**

Ease is a calm, beautifully designed app that helps you manage your time, work and energy so you can prepare for exams and presentations with less pressure.

## Features
- **Time management:** plan your preparation with flexible options.
- **Work and energy balance:** pace yourself and avoid burnout.
- **Liquid glass design:** a modern, Apple-style interface with clear contrast in light mode.
- **Fluid motion:** smooth, bouncy animations, a boot animation and a short in-app introduction.
- **Easy navigation:** a clean vertical sidebar.
